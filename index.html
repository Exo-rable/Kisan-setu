Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Kisan Setu

How do I register on Kisan Setu?

Registration is simple! Click on 'Get Started' button on the homepage, fill in your details (name, phone, email, state), and verify your mobile number with the OTP sent to you. You'll receive a farmer ID within minutes.

How accurate is the AI disease detection feature?

Our AI model has 98% accuracy after testing on over 50,000 crop images. For best results, upload clear photos of the affected area in natural light. If you're unsure, you can also consult with our expert agronomists for confirmation.

Are the mandi prices updated in real-time?

Yes, our mandi prices are updated every hour from major agricultural markets across India. Prices are sourced from official market websites and updated in real-time to give you the most current information for selling decisions.

How do I apply for government schemes through Kisan Setu?

Navigate to the 'Schemes' section, find the scheme you're eligible for, click 'Apply Now', and our platform will guide you through the application process. We'll help you fill the required forms and submit them to the relevant government department.

Is my personal and financial data secure?

Absolutely! Kisan Setu uses bank-level encryption and follows GDPR and Indian data protection standards. Your data is stored securely and is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

Can I access Kisan Setu on my mobile phone?

Yes! Kisan Setu is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones. We also have dedicated iOS and Android apps available in the App Store and Google Play Store for seamless mobile experience.
